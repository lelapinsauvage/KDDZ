# Kiddz Online Brand Direction Evaluation

**Date:** 2026-07-13
**Status:** Research shortlist and controlled finalist proof complete; final brand lock open
**Live evidence:** `/design-lab/brand-directions/evaluation`
**Final proof:** `/design-lab/brand-directions/finalists`
**Type/color readiness:** `docs/redesign/finalist-type-color-readiness.md`
**Identity-world proof:** `/design-lab/brand-directions/identity-worlds`
**Identity-world dossier:** `docs/redesign/finalist-identity-worlds.md`
**Production redesign:** Paused

This dossier pressure-tests the six V2 creative directions. It ranks strategic
potential; it does not claim that an unlaunched asset is already distinctive,
and it does not select production tokens.

## Method

The evaluation combines four evidence types:

1. The manager, practitioner, parent, compliance, parity, and workflow evidence
   already documented in the redesign program.
2. The same controlled Riverside nursery state rendered in all six directions.
3. Screen-level benchmark observations from Mobbin and official design systems.
4. A fixed seven-criterion expert heuristic weighted to 100 points.

The scores are deliberately transparent and falsifiable. They are not a
substitute for operator comprehension testing, recognition testing, or legal
clearance. Ehrenberg-Bass defines a true distinctive asset through uniqueness
and fame; these directions can only be scored for **distinctive-asset
potential** before real audience testing.

## Benchmark Findings

### Emotional systems

- [Headspace continuation](https://mobbin.com/screens/1d84c1c5-79dc-4765-9358-54d427102fd5)
  gives an illustration the emotional job while progress, labels, and navigation
  remain quiet on white.
- [Headspace daily sequence](https://mobbin.com/screens/ec5baa02-aace-497d-ac04-bfa0557e1bdd)
  uses one orange timeline signal, distinct media, and a clear sequence instead
  of distributing brand color through every control.
- [Duolingo progress](https://mobbin.com/screens/6a27582d-203a-4d59-b727-48f2634574eb)
  makes color describe progress while incomplete structure recedes.
- [Duolingo next action](https://mobbin.com/screens/821e9808-bc92-4c14-8003-d32191db87e5)
  uses a large branded progress object and one explicit action without turning
  the entire screen into a rainbow.

**Implication for Kiddz:** warmth needs an owned focal asset. Routine product
chrome should stay neutral so consequence, completion, imagery, and the focal
asset remain legible.

### High-capability systems

- [Revolut account overview](https://mobbin.com/screens/7d7bfcdf-486d-49a7-b831-08d5c13b07a8)
  organizes a broad product around one primary object, nearby contextual
  actions, and a restrained transaction hierarchy.
- [Revolut feature world](https://mobbin.com/screens/98f0802f-eb30-43c6-b97a-6a77c9244900)
  permits a vivid feature-level visual world while keeping the records below it
  highly conventional.
- [Notion task workspace](https://mobbin.com/screens/9aed23d0-f039-4685-aeb1-8a0f81628796)
  keeps a dense desktop surface content-first and reserves color for status.
- [Notion grouped work](https://mobbin.com/screens/56c3de49-067b-4f6c-a136-53fa7b15fb73)
  shows that deep capability can remain readable through hierarchy, alignment,
  and contextual controls rather than large decorative containers.
- [Vercel project overview](https://mobbin.com/screens/6410a8f7-a1dc-4e36-8db4-ef1ffc95049d)
  keeps navigation and empty states quiet while a pale checklist owns the
  current setup consequence.
- [Vercel live project](https://mobbin.com/screens/3784e0bf-583f-43a5-9c7f-237b31d88ac6)
  makes the production object, readiness, and immediate actions primary.

**Implication for Kiddz:** the desktop product needs one primary operational
object per view. Brand expression should intensify around completion, guidance,
parent trust, and selected moments rather than compete with daily scanning.

### Visual-culture systems

- [Cosmos visual feed](https://mobbin.com/screens/90c8e373-5923-4b13-a781-b31f16a70dd7)
  lets content create the atmosphere and uses almost no interface color.
- [Cosmos collection action](https://mobbin.com/screens/914d7f1e-5906-48b0-bc5d-5be292697097)
  introduces a conventional, high-contrast action sheet over expressive media.

**Implication for Kiddz:** image or illustration systems can be expressive, but
operational controls should remain familiar and exact.

## Scoring Constitution

| Criterion | Weight | Question |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Operational truth | 20% | Does it make safety, consequence, ownership, and evidence immediately credible? |
| Distinctive-asset potential | 18% | Could a small, repeatable asset family become recognizably Kiddz? |
| Emotional resonance | 16% | Does it add warmth and energy without infantilizing the manager? |
| Product scalability | 16% | Can it carry dense desktop operations, long forms, records, and parent moments? |
| Hierarchy and accessibility | 12% | Can color remain meaningful, contrast-safe, and secondary to content? |
| Motion character | 8% | Can purposeful transitions become recognizable and respect reduced motion? |
| Brand-world breadth | 10% | Can it stretch across product, parent trust, launch, guidance, and awards material? |
| **Total** | **100%** | |

Each direction receives an integer score from one to five per criterion. The
weighted total is `score / 5 * criterion weight`.

## Finalists

### Kinetic Kindness

**Why it advances:** it is the only system already anchored to an approved,
coherent identity seed: the O-ring, purposeful dot, sequential letter arrival,
and governed color constellation. It has the strongest emotional and motion
range for product, parents, onboarding, completion, and launch.

**Critical risk:** it can become a children's entertainment product if every
surface moves or every color appears at once.

**Must prove next:** a restrained desktop operating mode where white and ink
dominate, status has semantic color, and the expressive identity appears only
at meaningful moments.

**Asset-building order:** O-ring first, then purposeful dot, then one governed
motion signature, then the color constellation. One strong asset is more useful
than six simultaneous motifs.

### Living Record

**Why it advances:** it makes the core product promise tangible. Time, source,
authorship, care, and evidence become a humane visual language rather than
compliance furniture. It is particularly credible for incidents, handovers,
profiles, parent communication, and inspection.

**Critical risk:** editorial pacing can slow live comparison and high-volume
action.

**Must prove next:** a dense operational mode that retains the record's humanity
without adding ornamental headlines, rules, or publication metaphors.

**Asset-building order:** dated care mark, source-caption grammar, humane record
rhythm, then editorial type at selected reading moments.

## Challenger

### Care Commons

Care Commons remains useful because it tests a different truth: the nursery is a
coordinated social system. It has the strongest parent and team storytelling
potential, but it must work without portraits and must never blur the single
owner of a consequential action. Its connection mark and paired-role grammar
should be judged independently from photography.

## Held Directions

- **Open Studio:** strong launch identity and ownability; insufficient evidence
  that its graphic force improves high-stakes nursery decisions.
- **Bright Signal:** strongest operational grammar; weakest category
  differentiation from fintech and developer tooling. Its now/next model should
  survive as product logic even if the identity does not.
- **Quiet Magic:** strongest restraint and long-session usability; too dependent
  on borrowed minimalist signals to own Kiddz memory. Its restraint should
  survive as a product mode, not as the identity.

Holding a direction does not mean deleting its best product lesson. It means the
direction should not set the brand constitution in its current form.

## Decision Protocol

1. Review all six unscored specimens first.
2. Review this evidence and the two finalists second.
3. Review the completed controlled proof at
   `/design-lab/brand-directions/finalists`; its operations, incident, and parent
   surfaces preserve the same source content and behavior for both finalists,
   while its readiness appendix exposes type roles, 200% specimen behavior,
   French, Arabic RTL, mixed numerals, and semantic color.
4. Review `/design-lab/brand-directions/identity-worlds`; it keeps the logo seed,
   source meaning, application jobs, and decision criteria constant while
   comparing memory asset, voice, image grammar, motion, and brand expression.
5. Select Kinetic Kindness or Living Record. Do not create a hybrid by taste.
6. After the lock, codify the brand constitution before production components:
   purpose, promise, personality, distinctive assets, typography, color roles,
   illustration/imagery, voice, motion, accessibility, and do-not rules.
7. Test the chosen constitution on the same three proof surfaces: live
   operations, a high-trust record, and a parent-facing moment.

## Open Gate

No production winner is selected. Kinetic Kindness is the research lead after
the controlled proof; Living Record remains the evidence-led alternative. This
is not a production selection.
Component APIs, final tokens, production page migration, and brand-asset rollout
remain paused until the brand lock is made.
