# Creative Selection Gate

**Status:** Decision packet complete; explicit production selection open
**Review surface:** `/design-lab/territories`
**Verifier:** `src/scripts/verify-redesign-territory-selection.ts`
**Production UI changed:** No

## Decision Required

Select one production creative constitution:

1. **Daylight** - expressive warmth with concentrated operational color;
2. **Signal** - live precision and dense operating grammar;
3. **Carebook** - editorial care and record continuity.

The three prototypes use identical Riverside state, rooms, work, Children, Daily care, and Safety review flows. Selecting a territory therefore chooses expression, not a different feature set.

## Recommendation

Advance **Daylight**.

It earns the highest weighted score, `89.9 / 100`, and is the only direction that leads on both distinctive Kiddz ownership and emotional fit while remaining above the operational-clarity and accessibility gates. It is closest to the approved logo identity and the user's Headspace-like emotional reference without turning the manager product into a children's interface.

If selected, Daylight remains the coherent system. Two product grammars may be reinterpreted within its tokens:

- Signal's numeric alignment and source/timing discipline for ratios, attendance, finance, medical, audit, and other dense work;
- Carebook's record-first language for handovers, incidents, acknowledgment, and parent-facing narrative evidence.

This is not permission to blend Signal's technical shell or Carebook's paper/serif treatment into the master language.

## Evidence Summary

| Direction | Weighted score | Strongest evidence | Primary cost |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Daylight | 89.9 | Fastest readiness comprehension, strongest identity and emotional fit | Dense records need stricter alignment discipline |
| Carebook | 87.0 pre-fix | Best handover, incident, acknowledgment, and narrative continuity | Highest repeated-action, localization, and typography cost |
| Signal | 86.8 | Best room comparison, source/timing grammar, and dense surfaces | Too generic and emotionally cool as the master Kiddz language |

Carebook's original `3.8` accessibility score remains in the evaluation. The prototype was remediated and now passes the same computed/runtime floor, but the original failure remains implementation-cost evidence rather than being retroactively erased.

## What Selection Locks

- brand hierarchy and expression levels;
- product and display type roles;
- light, dark, and high-contrast token direction;
- color concentration and semantic-state behavior;
- radius, border, elevation, density, and spatial rules;
- motion character and spring/continuity tokens;
- component anatomy and production visual language.

## What Selection Cannot Change

- restored features or parity obligations;
- effective permissions or record scope;
- database truth and workflow state;
- legacy PHP aliases and output behavior;
- parent API and native parser contracts;
- accessibility, localization, responsive, reliability, and performance gates;
- the ban on fake charts, decorative blobs, colored left borders, nested cards, and generic component-kit assembly.

## Evidence Boundary

The selection surface uses real captured prototype screens, seven weighted criteria, the provisional ranking, explicit tradeoffs, and links to every interactive prototype. Existing browser evidence covers desktop, tablet, 390px, 320px, long English, Arabic RTL, deterministic 200% text, forced colors, reduced motion, and automated semantics.

Actual browser zoom, screen readers, real OS forced colors/reduced motion, color-vision simulation, native devices, nursery operators, and field performance remain later acceptance gates. Those limits do not require a fourth visual territory; they remain mandatory tests for the selected system.

No production direction is selected until the user explicitly chooses **Daylight**, **Signal**, or **Carebook**. After that decision, the next work is the brand/design constitution, final tokens, component APIs, and the desktop-first shell/Today pilot.
