=== Phase 27 — Research: Typography for Nursery Apps — Sat Feb 28 09:45:15 CET 2026 ===
=============================================
Committed. Here's a summary of the typography guide:

## Font Selection

| Role | Font | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| **Headings** | **Nunito** (600–800) | Rounded terminals give instant warmth. Gold standard for childcare apps — playful without being childish. |
| **Body + Arabic** | **Cairo** (300–700) | Native bilingual Latin + Arabic in one font. Geometric, modern, 9 weights. Designed by Egyptian foundry TitraShop. Handles compliance forms without script-switching hacks. |
| **Monospace** | **JetBrains Mono** (400–500) | Clear `0`/`O`/`1`/`l` differentiation for student IDs, invoice numbers, codes. |

## Key Decisions

- **Cairo solves the Arabic requirement** — it's a dual-script typeface where Latin and Arabic share harmonious metrics. When the UI renders ministry compliance forms in Arabic, the visual rhythm doesn't break.
- **Nunito only for headings** — its rounded terminals reduce body-text readability at 14px. Cairo takes over for everything below h4.
- **Total font budget: ~205 KB** (all variable fonts via Google Fonts CDN).
- Arabic text gets +1px size override, 1.7–1.8 line height (for diacritics), and zero letter-spacing (to preserve cursive connections).

The guide includes full type scale (h1–h6, body, caption, code), line-height and tracking specs, responsive mobile adjustments, CSS tokens, font loading strategy, and a table of 10 fonts we evaluated but rejected (with rationale).
